# S2 candidate screen — L-S2

**Disposition: no candidate survives the pre-outcome power and data gates.** No strategy signal, IC, portfolio return, or other outcome was computed. No pre-2020-11-27 price/return values and no sealed holdout data files were opened. The S&P holdout availability assessment below uses the existing data manifest only.

## Power gate applied before outcomes

The applicable synthetic S&P design from `report_drafts/R7_POWER_GATE.md` and `report_drafts/R7_POWER_GATE_STRESS.md` is a weekly rank-weighted top-half portfolio with a hard 50% total-notional turnover cap, 0.05% trading cost, and an equal-weight S&P benchmark. The conditional 80%-power route requires about **0.028 mean rank IC at signal persistence ρ=0.8**; the stress table gives modeled power of **27.5% at IC .020, 62.6% at .025, and 89.3% at .030** (Holm-27, synthetic planning only). At lower persistence (ρ=.6/.7), this portfolio misses the 50% active-share floor. The monthly capped rank-weighted design requires IC **.039/.045/.063** at ρ=.2/.5/.8; it also misses the active-share floor at ρ=.2/.5. These are design requirements, not fitted effects.

A published long-short return is not itself a rank IC. For a rough scale comparison only, pseudo-ICs below use the existing literature-note convention (spread divided by 2.8 × 11.31% monthly stock-volatility proxy); the proxy and conversions are not matched estimates and cannot qualify a candidate by themselves. Where the existing R7 power files have no candidate-matched IC, exact candidate P(pass) is reported as **not estimable**, with the relevant benchmark power shown. That is not treated as a power pass.

## Decision and governance

No candidate has both an outcome-free, defensible effect at the required design scale and the required point-in-time input/holdout path. **No S2 preregistration is drafted**; registering any of these now would lock an underpowered or unobservable hypothesis. No strategy outcome was calculated. The workspace's prior registered S2/J2 family rule remains: if a future S2 candidate is revived and may share the S&P holdout with J2, both rules must be frozen before either holdout test, added to one Holm family, and include all prior registered trials in the DSR count. The exact endpoint and DSR counts must be reconciled from the frozen ledgers at that time.
