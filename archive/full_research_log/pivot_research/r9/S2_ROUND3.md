**Decision: S2 exhausted; no candidate qualifies for a preregistration.** The broad-universe idea has a material survivorship/data-coverage blocker, and none of the reviewed post-2010 effect estimates clears the conservative cost-aware power screen with a complete, verified point-in-time input path. No signal, information coefficient, portfolio return, or other outcome was computed; no pre-2020-11-27 price, return, or volume values and no sealed holdout data were accessed.

## Universe and power design

The proposed point-in-time universe is the top 1,000 or 1,500 U.S. common stocks by trailing 60-session median dollar volume, measured only with sessions before each formation date. No index membership is used. Where industry controls or SIC2 peer groups are required, use dated SEC filing-header SIC exactly as in J1: SIC2 is the first two digits, available on the first U.S. session strictly after SEC acceptance, and carried only within the documented security/CIK lineage. The staged SIC panel starts in 2019, so 2016–2018 SIC2 coverage for this broader holdout is also unverified. The same deterministic builder must make development and the 2016-01–2020-10 holdout; its holdout output must be sealed with hashes and coverage/count metadata only. The benchmark is equal-weight of the same date-specific eligible universe and rebalances on the strategy schedule. For any later spec, a missing delisting return is fixed at −30% for performance delists and 0% for mergers/acquisitions; classify from exchange delisting/corporate-action notices or SEC filings, with unresolved events excluded from that classification and reported.

Alpaca's authenticated **GET `/v2/assets?asset_class=us_equity&status=active|inactive`** metadata calls returned 14,408 active records (13,221 on the major U.S. exchanges) and 19,152 inactive records, including 2,886 on the major exchanges. The fields are current symbol, exchange, name, tradability and status; there is no listing date, delisting date, or historical bar-availability field. The inactive symbols therefore show that Alpaca retains a substantial inactive-asset metadata list, but this metadata alone cannot establish that those symbols are queryable for 2016–2020 bars or identify which existed in that window. The count of historical names unavailable from Alpaca—and the survivorship-gap percentage—cannot be estimated from the permitted metadata. This fails the universe feasibility gate until a historical security master/bar-availability audit is authorized and completed. (No bars endpoint was called.)

The planning comparison uses the round-2 S&P baseline with T≈1,215 sessions, ρ=.8 (the only R7 rank-weighted Cap50 design with active share ≥50%), weekly/monthly rank-weighted top-half portfolios, 50% total-notional turnover cap, same-universe EW benchmark and a three-test holdout family (J2 + up to two S2). One-sided Holm's conservative first step is α=.05/3; the 80%-power normal multiplier is approximately 2.97. Breadth scales the R7 500-name hurdle by √(500/N). The cost input is tiered one-way half-spread assumptions of **5/10/20 bps** for high/medium/low liquidity terciles, applied to both strategy and EW benchmark trades on the same schedule. The tier ordering follows the published U.S. size-tercile spread evidence; 5/10/20 bps is a conservative planning assumption, not a measured project spread. At the 50% total-turnover cap and equal activity across terciles, this is approximately 5.8 bps of NAV per rebalance (about 3.0% annualized weekly or 0.7% monthly before benchmark-cost offset). The reported IC hurdles below are cost-aware planning approximations: the family-3, breadth-scaled R7 baseline is increased by the incremental spread drag versus R7's 5 bp per traded-dollar assumption. Costs are not estimated from market outcomes.
