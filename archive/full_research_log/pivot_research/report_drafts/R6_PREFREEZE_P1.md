## Exact fixes required

1. **Resolve the Dow warm-up conflict before freeze.** Move the first Dow formation to the first month-end for which 21 complete overnight observations can be built using only the permitted Dow development panel beginning 2020-11-27, and update the formation count and sample description consistently. Alternatively, the coordinator must explicitly stage and authorize a development-only warm-up input; do not read earlier Dow prices to fill this gap. Keep the S&P mechanism calendar aligned to the final registered formation set.
2. **Remove the cost floor from the mechanism pass gate.** H13 should require the goal-aligned registered direction and NW t≥2.00; H14 remains the placebo gate. The `break_even_rank_ic` result can remain a clearly labeled planning diagnostic, but cannot be an additional progression/pass condition. If the 10% dispersion assumption is retained as a diagnostic, give an independent rationale/source and state that it is not an empirical estimate from this project; do not select it after viewing outcomes. Clarify that the diagnostic's `5/30` approximation does not override `K=min(5,floor(n/2))` when the available Dow cohort is smaller.
3. **Pin down the Dow candidate cohort.** State whether a candidate must be in the PIT Dow roster at formation close, at execution open, or both. The defensible execution rule is the intersection of names with a valid formation-close ON21 signal and names in the PIT Dow roster at the following first open; use that same open-time roster for EW30 `n` and trade availability. Preserve only information known by the formation close plus opening-price availability at execution.
4. **Make the decision-63 sale rule operationally exact.** State that a stale holding is carried at its last valid close until the next scheduled monthly rebalance, sold at that rebalance's next valid open, and liquidated at its last valid close if no later valid price/open occurs. Specify how its weight/value is marked between the last close and that sale and apply the identical rule to EW30. Do not introduce an unscheduled rebalance or silently replace the name.
