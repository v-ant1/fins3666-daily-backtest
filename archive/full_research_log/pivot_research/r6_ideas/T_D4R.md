# T — D4 common-owner pressure reversal: external-universe screen

**Verdict: FAIL-GATE for confirmatory out-of-sample status; do not run as a confirmatory test.** A frozen test on non-Dow S&P 500 securities could provide a useful *entity-held-out replication* of D4's negative mechanism, because the D4 price and position panels covered Dow securities only. It is not a clean, independent confirmation: the reversal sign and test family are being proposed after seeing D4's negative result, the calendar and market regimes are the same, and the free 13F source panel for non-Dow names is not staged. No new signals, forward returns, ICs, portfolio outcomes, or holdout observations were computed for this note.

## What D4 already used

The frozen `specs/r4_prereg/D4.md` tested the positive-persistence hypothesis on 2020-11-27 through 2026-10-30. D4's input QA records 1,488 SPY trading dates, 36 unique securities across the point-in-time Dow rosters, 25 development 13F report-quarter cohorts (2020-06-30 through 2026-06-30), and 50 selected manager filings per cohort. Its position source was `r4_L4/data/13f_holder_by_dow_stock.csv`, filtered to report dates 2020-06-30 through 2026-09-30 and securities in the Dow roster; the 13F positions were usable only after filing availability. The saved signal bundle has at least five NCP scores on all 1,488 dates; the reported D4 primary IC used 1,479 finite date-wise cross-sections. D4's graph was nearly complete among Dow names (24 of 25 quarter graphs had all 435 possible pairs).

`R5_FINAL.md` and the independent `report_drafts/REPRO_D4.md` report the pre-registered positive-direction result: mean IC −0.018074, Newey–West *t* −2.41949 (lag 4), 1,479 dates. The independent reproduction matched. D4 did **not** run a Dow portfolio or holdout. These are already-seen outcomes and motivate the proposed negative sign; they cannot serve as independent confirmation.

The potential untouched rows are non-Dow S&P price/security rows and 13F position rows for securities outside D4's Dow-only position panel. The same dates, SEC source family, quarterly reporting calendar, and 13F-manager selection process are not untouched. In particular, using the same top-50 manager cohorts' non-Dow positions would leave substantial source-level dependence. A stronger split would use only non-Dow PIT S&P securities and, at each report quarter, exclude the contemporaneously selected D4 top-50 manager cohort from the new holdings graph; use only other public 13F filings, with each position entering after its filing becomes public. This split is not yet built or audited.

## Proposed one-variant mechanism registration (not frozen)

If retained for a limited cross-sectional replication, use exactly one variant and no parameter selection:

- **Universe and split:** point-in-time S&P 500 constituents minus the fixed union of all 36 security identifiers appearing in D4's development position panel. For every filing quarter, also exclude managers in that quarter's D4 point-in-time top-50 roster from the *new* graph. Use only the remaining public 13F holdings, mapped to non-Dow S&P names; no D4 NCP, graph-weight, or return values are copied. This is an entity-and-filing-row holdout, not a date holdout.
- **Signal:** at close *t*, calculate each eligible stock's raw CLV `(2C-H-L)/(H-L)` (zero if `H=L`), five-session arithmetic mean including *t*, then its D4-style row-normalized common-owner peer mean using only filing-available 13F positions. Use the same value-weighted manager portfolio/cosine construction as D4, but only on the eligible non-Dow nodes and non-D4 manager filings. Make no extra industry, size, volume, or return-correlation adjustment.
- **Prediction:** higher peer buying pressure predicts *lower* next-week returns, as a reversal. The primary dependent return is adjusted close at *t+5* divided by adjusted open at *t+1*, minus one. The primary statistic is the mean date-wise Spearman rank IC of NCP and that forward return; sign requirement is mean IC < 0 and Bartlett Newey–West *t* ≤ −2 at lag 4. For economic relevance, mirror the old D4 advance floor and require mean IC ≤ −0.0183. The 0.0183 magnitude is a conservative inherited D4 threshold, not a literature-derived reversal estimate.
- **Placebos:** before any run, freeze the D4 placebo definitions on this disjoint node/manager panel: (i) 500 directed degree-preserving rewires, (ii) 500 random-link graphs with the observed edge count, and (iii) the graph shifted by exactly 20 sessions. Use one `default_rng(20261111)` stream and the D4 mechanics; require the observed *negative* IC to be below the fifth percentile of each randomized null and each left-tail randomization *p* < .05. Keep all placebo draw outputs. Register all primary/placebo inferential endpoints in the R6 Holm family before outcomes; no dropping failed tests.
- **Stopping rule:** a mechanism result can only be called a non-Dow S&P replication. It does not authorize a Dow development portfolio: every Dow price date has already entered D4's development result. Any later trade test needs a separately authorized coordinator-only holdout procedure, and must be treated as a new test with its own registered family and data gate.

## Gates (a)–(e)

**(a) Literature, verified — PASS for reversal/common-owner links, not for the proposed CLV statistic.** Antón and Polk, [“Connected Stocks” (*Journal of Finance*, 2014)](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12149), connect stocks through common active mutual-fund ownership and document cross-stock reversal strategies. Lehmann, [“Fads, Martingales, and Market Efficiency” (*Quarterly Journal of Economics*, 1990)](https://doi.org/10.2307/2937816), documents short-horizon return reversal. These papers support a reversal hypothesis, but neither tests D4's five-day peer CLV-NCP on a disjoint S&P universe. Recent adjacent evidence is mixed: Choi and Kim, [“Short-term Momentum” (*Review of Financial Studies*, 2022)](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhab055), find reversal for low-turnover stocks but momentum for high-turnover stocks, and report their reversal is strongest among large, liquid and extensively covered stocks. That result is own-stock return sorting, not a common-owner buying-pressure network.

**(b) Mega-cap / post-2020 survival — PARTIAL, not established for this mechanism.** The 2022 RFS evidence is a reason not to dismiss short-term reversal solely because the firms are large; Antón–Polk provide a common-owner cross-stock reversal mechanism. D4 itself found a negative peer-pressure IC in the Dow during 2020–2026, but that is the discovery result under review, not an independent post-2020 confirmation. No verified paper establishes that the *same five-day peer-CLV / 13F signal* reverses in a post-2020 mega-cap or non-Dow S&P cross-section. Also, CLV is a price-location proxy rather than signed order flow, and 13F is a delayed holdings snapshot.

**(c) Power — conditional PASS at a hypothetical effect; optimistic for the actual design.** I ran the synthetic `r4_harness/power_sim.py` with 449 names (the observed median number of daily price-available ex-D4 roster members), 1,488 dates, horizon 5/NW lag 4, assumed serial correlation 0.80, assumed date-level IC SD 0.08, and a *direction-adjusted* planted mean IC of +0.0183 (equivalent to raw IC −0.0183). With 5,000 simulations and seed 20261111, estimated power for NW *t* ≥ 2 was 0.9454 (Monte Carlo SE 0.0032), effective observations 410.9, and 80%-power MDE 0.01121. This is a design sensitivity only: the IC SD and autocorrelation are assumptions, not estimates from this proposed panel; the simulator does not model network/manager clusters, variable cross-sections, the 13F graph's slow update cadence, multiple-testing adjustment, or placebo gates. Treat the number as an upper-bound planning check, not evidence that the effect is detectable in practice.

**(d) Free-data feasibility — FAIL at the network-input gate; price coverage is promising.** The input-only check of `r6_data/sp500_ohlcv_dev_gapfilled.parquet` read just date/ticker identifiers. On the D4 date interval (2020-11-27 through 2026-10-30), the active PIT roster has 503–505 names; after excluding D4's 36-security union, 428–469 names per date had price rows (median 449, 90th percentile 463, maximum 469) across all 1,488 dates. These are coverage counts, not checks of price values or return completeness. The local D4 position file is Dow-security-only, so there is no local non-Dow holdings graph or network-coverage count to report. SEC structured [Form 13F datasets](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/form-13f-data-sets) are free and provide a route to pull other public filers' holdings; filings are quarterly and positions must be lagged to their actual public filing dates ([SEC Form 13F FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-13f)). However, CUSIP-to-issuer mapping, manager exclusions, amendments, incomplete reporting, and non-Dow link density still need a development-only build and coverage QA before this gate could pass. No such pull was run here.

**(e) Novelty / genuinely untouched — FAIL for confirmatory novelty.** A non-Dow/non-D4-manager row split is technically different from D4's Dow-only price rows and top-50 manager holdings. But the reversal sign is selected because D4 returned a negative result; the signal, horizon, graph family, calendar, market regimes, and motivating outcome are shared. Freezing now prevents further tuning but does not undo post-result hypothesis selection. The most accurate label would be “post-hoc, entity-held-out replication of a D4-motivated reversal,” not independent confirmation or a new strategy direction.

## Red-team conclusion

The asset/date rows described above were not part of D4's mechanism calculation; thus a carefully built ex-Dow, ex-D4-manager data panel could test whether the D4 reversal generalizes to new issuers. That narrow separation is real. The proposed test nevertheless is not genuinely out-of-sample in the inferential sense requested: the reversal was noticed after the D4 result, the same development dates and broad market shocks are reused, and the independent 13F graph is not currently available locally. **Final verdict: FAIL-GATE.** Do not call it a new pre-registered discovery. At most, after a coordinator freezes the exact split, endpoints, manager-exclusion rule, and source-data hashes before building or viewing any signal/return statistic, it could be reported as a transparent external-universe replication with post-selection disclosure. It cannot validate a Dow portfolio or bypass the sealed-holdout procedure.
