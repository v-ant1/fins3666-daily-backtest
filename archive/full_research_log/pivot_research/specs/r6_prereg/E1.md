## Hypothesis and fixed design

**Mechanism.** Positive, volume-weighted close-location pressure in one company persists or diffuses to the returns of its economically related same-industry peers. Estimate the mechanism on the point-in-time S&P 500 membership panel in `r6_data`; after the mechanism gate, trade a monthly top-five Dow 30 portfolio against point-in-time EW30.

**Primary sign and horizon.** Positive cross-sectional rank IC between a stock's preceding-month leave-one-out same-industry peer-pressure score and its next 21-trading-session total return. There is one primary score, one positive sign, and one horizon. No negative-sign result, alternate horizon, alternate industry classification, or alternate pressure definition can rescue a failure.

### Point-in-time industry graph

- At each monthly formation close `t`, use the S&P 500 point-in-time membership effective on that date, from `r6_data/sp500_pit_roster.csv`.
- The sole industry classification is **two-digit SIC**: the first two numeric digits of the issuer's SEC SIC code in the latest 10-K filing whose `sic_usable_from` date is on or before `t`. `sic_usable_from` is the first U.S. equity trading session strictly after the SEC filing acceptance date (acceptance timestamp is converted to U.S. Eastern calendar date; if accepted on a trading day, usable next trading day; if accepted on a non-trading day, usable the next trading day). Thus a filing accepted on formation date is not usable at that close. Carry a classification from its `sic_usable_from` until a later 10-K classification becomes usable. Apply this rule to historical SIC and any explicitly flagged current-SIC fallback; a fallback is usable only from its documented availability date, never carried backward. Use corresponding historical CIK/ticker lineage. If filing/availability dating cannot be reconstructed, the input gate fails.
- Link two distinct, in-roster firms at `t` if and only if their known two-digit SIC codes match. Links are undirected, binary, and have zero self-links. Group size is the number of current valid members with that SIC. At least two non-target peers must be present for a target score; otherwise that target is ineligible that month. Do not merge small groups, broaden SIC codes, or switch to GICS.

### Signal, score and return definition

For each stock `j` and each of the 21 trading sessions ending at formation close `t`, compute daily close-location value:

```text
CLV[j,d] = 0,                                      if high[j,d] = low[j,d]
           (2*close[j,d] - high[j,d] - low[j,d])
             / (high[j,d] - low[j,d]),              otherwise
```

Use the internally consistent daily OHLC fields; the ratio is scale invariant. Daily traded dollar value is `close[j,d] * volume[j,d]`. The exact 21-session stock pressure is:

```text
P[j,t] = sum_d(CLV[j,d] * close[j,d] * volume[j,d])
         / sum_d(close[j,d] * volume[j,d]),
         d = the 21 sessions ending at t.
```

If any daily OHLC or volume input is missing, that firm's pressure is missing for the month; do not fill it. The target score is the **equal-weight mean** of available same-SIC peer pressures, excluding the target itself:

```text
S[i,t] = mean(P[j,t] for j != i with dated SIC2[j,t] = SIC2[i,t]).
```

The primary S&P 500 outcome is the date-wise Spearman correlation between `S[i,t]` and each eligible security's next-21-session adjusted total return, measured from the next session's open through the close of its 21st session. Adjusted open is `raw_open * adjusted_close / raw_close` on that session; the forward return is `adjusted_close[t+21] / adjusted_open[t+1] - 1`. Use only scores, memberships, and SEC SIC information available by `t`; no same-day close-to-close return enters the forecast return. Rank IC is calculated separately on each monthly formation date, then inference is on this time series.

Formation is the last U.S. equity trading session of each calendar month. The score is known at that close and trades at the next session's open. Hold the resulting portfolio until the next month's scheduled open rebalance. The scheduled development dates are monthly from 2020-11-27 through 2026-10-30; the last observed month end, 2026-10-30, is excluded from confirmatory 21-session forward-return tests because the development price calendar contains no full subsequent 21-session window. Publish the full scheduled-date and forward-eligibility mask before any outcome calculation; eligibility is determined solely from the development trading calendar, never observed returns.

## Mechanism pass rule and IC hurdle

The sole confirmatory mechanism endpoint is the mean monthly S&P 500 rank IC. Compute its Newey–West t-statistic with Bartlett weights and **lag 20**. E1 advances only if both conditions hold:

1. Mean rank IC is positive and at least **0.001155**; and
2. the one-sided Newey–West statistic is **t ≥ 2.00**.

The cost-only IC threshold comes from `r4_harness.phase3.break_even_rank_ic` with the frozen design assumptions `turnover=0.20`, `return_dispersion=0.06`, `cost_per_side=0.0005`, and `top_fraction=5/30`:

```text
break_even_rank_ic(0.20, 0.06, 0.0005, 5/30) = 0.0011547005
```

The 20% monthly total-notional turnover is the planning input and portfolio cap; it is a turnover target, not a measured result. The 6% monthly cross-sectional return dispersion is an explicit planning assumption, not estimated from the project data. The conversion is for the top-five Dow portfolio and is not an estimated alpha. The signal must meet the same threshold on the broader S&P 500 mechanism test, and the actual Dow net-portfolio gate remains separate.

The existing synthetic power design for about 71 monthly dates, horizon 21, 500 assets, AR(1) signal correlation 0.20, and 2,000 simulations gives an 80%-power detectable mean IC of about 0.01831 and approximately 48.5% power at a planted IC of 0.010. This is an assumption-based planning result from `r4_harness/power_sim.py`, not a finding on project outcomes; peer clustering and SIC missingness could make power lower. Report this limitation alongside the S&P500 test. Do not relax t≥2 or the IC hurdle if the test is underpowered.

## Placebo

Run and report the fixed placebo regardless of the primary result. Use seed `20261111` and 500 independent date-wise permutations. On each formation date, randomly reassign the observed two-digit SIC labels across the frozen set of eligible S&P 500 members, preserving the exact SIC2 count/group-size multiset on that date. Rebuild leave-one-out peer scores and recompute the mean monthly rank IC for each draw. The observed primary mean IC must exceed the 95th percentile of the 500 shuffled mean-IC values, and the one-sided randomization p-value `(1 + number(null_mean_IC >= observed_mean_IC))/501` must be below 0.05. This is the sole placebo endpoint and tests whether the actual dated industry assignment adds information beyond date-wise group sizes. The prior 21-session stale-signal zero-equivalence test is removed: persistence could make a stale score predictive, so equivalence to zero is not a valid null for this hypothesis. Do not tune the seed, eligible mask, grouping, or null rule after seeing outcomes.

## Dow 30 portfolio, costs and inference

Only after the input, mechanism, and shuffled-industry placebo gates pass, run the frozen strategy on the point-in-time Dow 30 development panel:

- At each monthly formation close, rank the eligible Dow names by the same E1-V1 peer-pressure score and set a long-only, equal-weight target in the top **K=5** names. No shorts, cash target, factor tilt, or alternate K is permitted.
- Start from point-in-time EW30. At the next-session open, move from current weights toward the target by the largest pro-rata step that keeps **buys plus sells divided by NAV at or below 20%** for that rebalance. Apply this 20% cap from the first transition onward; it is stricter than the R6 hard limit of 50%. If a score is unavailable, do not impute it; leave its target weight at zero and renormalize over eligible names only if at least five are eligible, otherwise the portfolio gate fails that date and is reported.
- Mark daily portfolio values and compare with point-in-time EW30 over the identical eligible names. Charge **0.05% per dollar of total traded notional** (buys plus sells counted once). Include the cost of the initial EW30-to-target transition. Report the actual average and maximum turnover, portfolio active share against EW30, number of eligible names, and the dates the cap binds. Require average active share **≥50%** and actual turnover **≤50% on every rebalance**. The 20% cap is the specified implementation; do not loosen it if the portfolio becomes too close to EW30.
- The net portfolio must have positive cumulative excess return and a higher annualized net Sharpe than EW30. Report the realized cost/gross-edge ratio (diagnostic; the goal's turnover-or-cost rule is met by the ≤50% turnover cap). Use a one-sided circular block bootstrap with **5,000 draws, 10-session blocks, and seed 20261111** for net excess return; both raw and Holm-adjusted p-values must be below 0.05. Report gross/net returns, net Sharpe, drawdown, active share, turnover, costs, cost/gross-edge ratio, raw and adjusted p-values, and DSR.

The portfolio is a hurdle, not an automatic consequence of S&P500 mechanism success. If initial staging under the turnover cap prevents average active share of 50%, report failure; do not waive the active-share rule or exclude transition costs.
