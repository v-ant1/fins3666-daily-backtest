# D5 — directed same-industry leader-to-follower pressure

STATUS: READY FOR FREEZE AFTER CHECKLIST — NOT FROZEN. No D5 outcome data have been computed.

This is a falsifiable test of whether short-horizon buying pressure at the most actively traded Dow name in a dated SIC industry predicts subsequent returns among its industry peers. It uses point-in-time industry links rather than a correlation graph. Dollar volume ranks leaders; it is not itself interpreted as investor attention or causal transmission.

## Hypothesis, sign and horizon

At a signal close, positive five-session close-location-value (CLV) pressure at a same-industry leader predicts positive next-week returns among its followers. Reversing the directed edges should remove the effect. The only primary horizon is five trading sessions.

## Inputs, industry and directed graph

- **Universe and period:** point-in-time Dow 30 roster and daily OHLCV, development dates 2020-11-27 through 2026-10-30. Use the dated issuer-SIC observations in `r4_L4/data/sic_panel.csv`; this input must be completed and audited before freeze. Never substitute the current SEC SIC snapshot for the dated panel.
- **Industry level:** parse the issuer's four-digit SIC as a four-character code, retaining leading zeroes; the industry key is exactly the first two digits (the SIC major group). Do not use SIC divisions, three-digit groups or four-digit industries. For signal session `t`, use the latest issuer 10-K header SIC with an SEC acceptance timestamp no later than 16:00 America/New_York on the immediately preceding NYSE session. Record that timestamp. A filing accepted after the prior-session cutoff, including any filing accepted on session `t`, enters the graph only from the next eligible session. Carry the latest prior valid code forward; do not backfill. Missing or invalid SIC means no edge.
- **Leader ranking:** on signal date `t`, compute each active member's mean daily dollar volume from unadjusted close × reported share volume over exactly `t−20` through `t−1`. For the 60-session sensitivity, use exactly `t−60` through `t−1`. Rank only active names within the same two-digit SIC. Highest mean dollar volume is the sole leader; break exact ties by ticker in ascending ASCII order. Create a directed unit-weight edge from that leader to every other active Dow member in the same SIC. No self-edge, thresholding or within-industry alternative is allowed. A singleton SIC contributes no edge. Ranking inputs are known by the signal date and never use outcomes.
- **Pressure and score:** `CLV_i,t = (2×close_i,t − high_i,t − low_i,t)/(high_i,t − low_i,t)`; set it to zero when high equals low. Leader pressure is the arithmetic mean of its CLV over `t−4:t`. Follower score is the mean pressure of its incoming leaders; in this one-leader-per-industry graph that is the leader's five-session pressure. The graph and score at `t` use only information public by the signal close. No score is assigned to a leader without incoming edges.
- **Pre-outcome graph feasibility gate:** at each eligible fifth-session formation date, count distinct ordered stock pairs, distinct leader firms, and distinct two-digit SIC groups represented by an edge. D5 is declared **infeasible before any future return, IC, event response or portfolio result is calculated** if fewer than 10 distinct pairs, fewer than 3 leaders, or fewer than 3 SIC groups are present on more than 20% of eligible dates. Do not broaden the industry level, change the window, pool non-Dow assets or lower these thresholds. Report all three counts and distributions from input coverage only; do not relax the thresholds after the coverage check.
- **Known data limitation:** at drafting, the dated SIC panel is still being built and its date/ticker coverage and the above pair-count gate are unverified. This preregistration cannot be frozen until the panel, SIC parsing/date rules and gate are checked without computing outcomes.

## Primary mechanism test and placebos

On each eligible date, calculate cross-sectional Spearman rank IC between the follower score and the follower's next-open-to-close five-session return, `adjusted_close[t+5] / adjusted_open[t+1] − 1`. Report the date-level IC series, its mean and Newey–West t-statistic with Bartlett lag 4. The primary mechanism passes only if mean IC ≥ 0.0183 and NW t ≥ 2.0. Also report the raw number of eligible observations and effective dates; no alternative horizon can replace a failed primary.

Use 500 within-SIC leader permutations, seed `20261110`. Keep two deterministic negative controls: the reverse-edge graph, and the dated graph shifted exactly 20 trading sessions (one fixed shift; no random shift draws). Preserve the dated roster and industry member counts in permutations. For the permutation placebo, report its distribution, one-sided randomization p-value and observed-statistic percentile. The true variant-1 IC must exceed the 95th percentile of the permutation distribution, and the reverse-edge, permuted-link and shifted-graph IC series must each have absolute Newey–West t < 2.00. Report all three checks, whether or not the primary passes. A failed primary mechanism test ends D5; no portfolio test follows.

## Variants and selection rule

1. **Primary:** 20-session trailing mean dollar-volume leader; two-digit SIC; five-session mean CLV; five-session next-open-to-close outcome.
2. **Fixed sensitivity:** 60-session trailing mean dollar-volume leader with all other rules unchanged.
Variant 1 alone determines advancement. Variant 2 is reported in full and cannot replace a failed primary or be selected for better returns. Reverse edges are one deterministic placebo check, not a separate variant or rescue test. The breadth feasibility gate applies to the primary graph before outcomes; the 60-session sensitivity does not change that gate.

## Portfolio, costs and inference

Only after a primary mechanism pass, rank eligible followers by score at each fifth-session close and buy the top five equal-weight at the next session's open. Hold through the next weekly formation; score ties break by ticker ascending. Compare with equal-weight among the point-in-time available Dow names. At formation, top-five active share is 83.3% when all 30 names are eligible. Cap total traded notional divided by NAV at 50% per rebalance; charge 0.05% per traded dollar with the harness actual-trade engine. If eligibility changes make the cap or 50% active-share condition impossible, report the condition and do not count the portfolio as a pass.

For the cost-only rank-IC scale reference, use benchmark-relative total-notional turnover of 0.50 NAV as the fixed helper input: `r4_harness.break_even_rank_ic(0.50, 0.03007510102898867, cost_per_side=0.0005, top_fraction=1/6) = 0.0057591`. This is a planning assumption, not measured D5 turnover and not the strategy's separate 50% NAV turnover limit; report actual strategy and EW30 traded notional and realized costs from the actual-trade engine. The advancement cost hurdle is mean rank IC ≥ 0.0183, the conservative top-five benchmark: the documented L1 weekly active turnover was 1.58751 NAV with five-session cross-sectional return dispersion 0.030075, making the 50%-turnover helper floor too low for a high-turnover top-five strategy. Require gross active edge at least four times realized costs, positive net excess return and higher net Sharpe than EW30.

For the portfolio endpoint, use a one-sided circular block bootstrap with 5,000 draws, 10-session blocks and seed `20261110`; report raw and Holm-adjusted p-values and the deflated Sharpe ratio at the full goal trial count of 30. The D5 endpoints entered into the complete D4–D6 Holm family are: (1) variant 1, 20-session primary five-session follower IC (one-sided Newey–West p-value); (2) reverse-edge mean IC (two-sided Newey–West p-value); (3) observed-versus-within-SIC permuted-leader placebo comparison (one-sided randomization p-value); (4) 20-session-shifted-graph mean IC (two-sided Newey–West p-value); (5) variant 2, 60-session five-session follower IC (one-sided Newey–West p-value); and (6) variant 1 net portfolio excess return versus EW30 (one-sided block-bootstrap p-value). Report raw and Holm-adjusted p-values for each. Count the reverse-edge calculation once as a placebo, not as a separate rescue variant. The coordinator records the full D4–D6 endpoint family in `specs/r4_trials.md` before any outcome is computed. Apply Holm once to the complete goal family, including all preregistered sensitivity tests; do not correct D5 in isolation. Report the full family and all failures. Portfolio pass requires Holm-adjusted p < 0.05.

## Sealed holdout procedure and pass rule

Only the coordinator may run one holdout job, once, after frozen code and this preregistration are verified. Use the point-in-time Dow roster over 2010-01-01 through 2020-10-31, with the gaps disclosed: KFT, DWDP and WBA are unavailable; UTX is proxied by its RTX lineage. Compute the EW benchmark over available names. Report the DWDP membership window both included and excluded. Do not impute missing names or alter this graph, signal, hurdle, horizon or execution rule for the holdout. Workers must not access holdout prices, returns or signals.

Full holdout pass requires mean five-session IC ≥ 0.0183 with positive sign, positive net excess return, Sharpe above EW over available names, and one-sided 10-session block-bootstrap p < 0.10; include the required DWDP sensitivity and disclose all gaps. Incomplete coverage is a limitation and cannot be represented as a complete Dow 30 test.
