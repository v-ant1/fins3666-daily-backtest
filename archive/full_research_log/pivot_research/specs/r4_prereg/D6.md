## Hypothesis and evidence

D6 tests whether lagged Treasury price pressure predicts equity returns and whether equity price pressure predicts Treasury returns in the opposite direction. The fixed primary prediction is positive bond-to-equity cross-prediction; the registered secondary prediction is negative equity-to-bond cross-prediction. ETF total-return trends are a slow price-pressure proxy, not observed flows or signed order flow. This is one fixed equity–Treasury link, not a general network.

Pitkäjärvi, Suominen and Vaittinen report monthly cross-asset trend predictability between bonds and equities ([Journal of Financial Economics, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2019.02.011)). Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen report time-series momentum across futures including equity-index and bond contracts ([Journal of Financial Economics, 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2011.11.003)). These studies motivate the direction and monthly horizon; they do not establish that this ETF design works.

## Primary mechanism regression and placebos

Estimate these two fixed monthly regressions:

`SPY_return[m+1] = alpha + beta_BE × B[m] + gamma_E × SPY_trend[m] + error`

`Treasury_return[m+1] = alpha + beta_EB × SPY_trend[m] + gamma_B × B[m] + error`

The primary is `beta_BE > 0`, with Newey–West Bartlett lag 11 and t ≥ 2.00. The secondary is `beta_EB < 0`; report its coefficient, standard error and Newey–West t-statistic, but it cannot rescue a failed primary. Including the target class's own trend makes the cross-lag an incremental prediction test.

Run 500 placebo draws with seed 20261110. For each draw, circularly shift the bond-basket signal B[m] relative to the SPY target returns by an integer offset chosen uniformly from 12 through N−12 months, where N is the number of valid primary observations; keep the SPY own-trend control and each series' internal order fixed. Report the placebo coefficient distribution, percentile and one-sided randomization p-value. The observed primary coefficient must exceed the 95th percentile of this distribution. Also report the reversed-direction regression and the target-own-trend-only regressions as fixed diagnostics; they cannot replace the registered primary test. A failed primary test ends D6 before any portfolio is evaluated.

## Variants, endpoints, and Holm family

Three variants are fixed:

1. **Primary:** 12-minus-1 Treasury-basket trend → next-month SPY return, positive.
2. **Secondary direction:** 12-minus-1 SPY trend → next-month equal-weight Treasury return, negative; it cannot advance D6.
3. **Sensitivity:** repeat both regressions with six-minus-one trends, `adjusted_close[a,m−1] / adjusted_close[a,m−6] − 1`; neither result can replace the primary.

The six D6 inferential endpoints entered into the round-wide Holm family are: (1) primary 12-minus-1 bond-to-SPY coefficient, one-sided `β_BE > 0`; (2) secondary 12-minus-1 SPY-to-Treasury coefficient, one-sided `β_EB < 0`; (3) primary observed-versus-circular-shift placebo comparison, one-sided; (4) six-minus-one bond-to-SPY coefficient, one-sided positive; (5) six-minus-one SPY-to-Treasury coefficient, one-sided negative; and (6) portfolio net excess return versus EW5, one-sided positive. Report raw and Holm-adjusted p-values for all six. The coordinator records the full family of every pre-registered D4–D6 endpoint, including each sensitivity and placebo comparison, in `specs/r4_trials.md` before any outcome calculation. Do not use a fixed test count or remove a failed endpoint. DSR uses `trial_count=30` for the whole goal.

## Portfolio, cost hurdle, and inference

Only after the primary mechanism and placebo gates pass, form five sign-adjusted monthly scores: SPY receives `B[m]`; each Treasury ETF receives `−SPY_trend[m]`. For asset a, define monthly total return k as `adjusted_close[a,k] / adjusted_close[a,k−1] − 1`, using the last available session close of each month. Divide SPY's score by the population standard deviation of the Treasury-basket's 12 monthly total returns for k=m−12 through m−1; divide each Treasury score by the population standard deviation of SPY's matching 12 monthly total returns. If a required standard deviation is missing or zero, mark the score unavailable. At each month-end, hold 100% in the ETF with the highest score, execute at the next month's first available open, and rebalance monthly. Break score ties in ascending ticker order. Compare with equal-weight EW5; formation active share is 80%. Report actual traded notional and costs from the harness actual-trade engine; do not impose an unimplemented turnover cap. Pass requires positive net excess return, net Sharpe above EW5, and gross active edge at least four times actual costs. Average turnover must be at most 50% of NAV per rebalance, or the four-times-cost requirement must hold.

The fixed planning cost hurdle is **10 basis points gross active return per monthly rebalance** at the 50% turnover boundary: 0.05% per traded dollar × 0.50 NAV turnover = 2.5 bp maximum cost, and the registered four-times-cost rule requires 10 bp gross edge. This is a planning hurdle; the portfolio test uses actual realized turnover and costs. Use the harness one-sided circular block bootstrap with 5,000 draws, 10-month blocks and seed 20261110. Report raw and round-wide Holm-adjusted p-values; portfolio pass requires adjusted p < 0.05. Report net excess, net Sharpe, turnover, costs, active share, cost/gross-edge ratio and DSR at trial count 30.
