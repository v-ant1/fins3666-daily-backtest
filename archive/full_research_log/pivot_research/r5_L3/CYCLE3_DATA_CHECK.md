# Cycle 3 ETF-flow input availability check

## Verdict: INFEASIBLE under the fixed gate

I checked only public input availability and date coverage for the six ETFs named in [CYCLE3_DIRECTION.md](CYCLE3_DIRECTION.md): SPY, IVV, VOO, QQQ, DIA, and IWM. No ETF prices, returns, portfolio outcomes, or holdout data were read or computed. I inspected only the schema and date column in two State Street history workbooks; no NAV, price, or return values were read.

The fixed gate requires daily shares outstanding/NAV/AUM for all six funds over at least 1,000 common sessions, and point-in-time holdings on at least 80% of sessions. It fails at both requirements: daily share-history rows were verified for only SPY and DIA, while SEC N-PORT is quarterly-public and issuer holdings pages reviewed did not provide a historical daily archive for the full six-fund set. The graph-density condition was not evaluated because the first two conjunctive gates already fail; no names or links were broadened to rescue the direction.

For the same reason, SEC filing counts by registrant CIK were not treated as fund-specific counts where a registrant contains many ETF series.

## Gate application

1. **Daily flows/share history for all six funds, at least 1,000 common sessions: FAIL.** SPY and DIA each have 1,488 daily issuer rows. No equivalent historical daily series was verified for IVV, VOO, QQQ, or IWM; quarterly N-PORT rows cannot replace the required daily data.
2. **Point-in-time basket coverage at least 80% of sessions: FAIL.** Issuer pages reviewed expose current daily holdings, but no historical daily archive was verified for the full six funds. The free SEC alternative supplies at most one publicly released quarter-end holdings snapshot per fund per quarter (up to 24 dates over this interval), not the required daily file history.
3. **Graph density and neighbor coverage: NOT EVALUATED.** The input-only gate is conjunctive, and it already fails. No graph was constructed.
4. **Source timestamp and field reconciliation: FAIL for the six-fund panel.** SPY and DIA issuer histories have the required share-count and assets schema, but the same daily fields and historical timestamp coverage were not established for all six funds.

**Final ruling: INFEASIBLE.** This is not a judgment about whether ETF flows affect constituent prices; it is a data-availability ruling under the preregistered coverage thresholds. The only compliant next step would be to stop this direction rather than lower the thresholds, forward-fill quarterly holdings as if they were daily files, or add funds after seeing the coverage gap.
