## Audit limits

Direct DOI links to several journal platforms intermittently returned access errors; in those cases I verified identity and the quoted claim against the linked author/NBER full text or publisher metadata. The audit does not imply that every paper’s result has been independently replicated, that the estimates survive current costs, or that the linked PDFs represent the final typeset version. Card 2’s exact sample count was not confirmed by text search in the reopened PDF, so I removed that number from the card and kept only the broad NYSE/AMEX panel description.
