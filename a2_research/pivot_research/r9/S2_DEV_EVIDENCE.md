# S2 development evidence — L-DEVEV

**Run date:** 2026-11-11 UTC. Exploratory development-only evidence; no holdout observations were opened. Seven execution attempts were counted across three registered tests (including two incomplete event implementations). The reported event results use the final exact-definition run. Development estimates are noisy and do not validate the planned holdout effects.

## Methods and limits

Effective dates come from the staged reconstructed PIT roster; dated announcement dates were read from freely accessible S&P source links present in that roster (104 addition links and 106 deletion links). Daily abnormal return is adjusted-close stock return minus same-day equal-weight return of PIT members present at both closes, excluding the event name; CAR is the arithmetic sum over the registered inclusive sessions. Mean-CAR confidence intervals use a two-sided event-level Student-t interval; same-date event clustering can make them optimistic. No event-window SD is reported where no CAR was observable.

The published S&P effects concern announcement-to-effective CAR. The only measurable event window is the addition post-effective reversal, so its SD-derived power requirement cannot establish power for the published effect. The N≈96 values are planning event counts supplied by the coordinator. The 2010–2020 scenario is footnote-only. No pre-2020-11-27 prices or returns were used.

For DTC, only FINRA rows with settlement dates on/after 2020-11-27 were used. With no publication timestamp in the panel, the signal was considered available seven U.S. federal business days after settlement; each month-end uses the latest released observation. Monthly cross-sections require PIT membership at both month-ends and at least 30 names. IC is Spearman; NW t uses a Bartlett kernel with three monthly lags. Returns use adjusted closes and omit dividends because the authorized bundle has no dividend series. Power MDE is `2.970 × SD(monthly IC) / √N`, with N≈58 primary. The published **1.19%** DTC spread is from a different sample/estimand and is shown for context only. FINRA publication timing is approximated, not timestamp-verified.

*Footnote (2010–2020 planning scenario only):* for the measured addition post-effective reversal SD, N≈216 gives an MDE of **1.70%**; for DTC dispersion, N≈130 gives an IC MDE of **0.0207**. These are planning scenarios, not estimates based on holdout data.\n\n**Overall:** None of the three leads has sufficient matched development evidence and estimable power for a preregistration; event leads are additionally blocked by the membership-masked event-price coverage, and DTC does not clear its primary IC MDE.
