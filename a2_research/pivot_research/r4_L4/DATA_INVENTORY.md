# Data inventory for pressure and network research

Checked 10 November 2026. This is a feasibility inventory, not a recommendation to trade. All sample evidence is restricted to the project development period, 2020-11-27 to 2026-10-30 (or the live 2026 development date for issuer holdings). No holdout prices, returns, or signals were examined. The public availability checks for the separate holdout task used only timestamp arrays and field names.

## Source comparison

| Source | Access and usable contents | Coverage / frequency | Cost | Effort | Verdict for this project |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SEC EDGAR Form 13F | SEC quarterly 13F filing archive/data sets; download XML or structured quarter files, map issuer CUSIPs to point-in-time security identifiers, then construct pairwise shared-holder weights. Filing instructions and records are public; the SEC APIs do not require an API key. | Institutional managers report quarter-end long positions, filed up to 45 days after quarter end; U.S. listed equities and some options. SEC structured 13F data starts in 2013 Q1; filings themselves extend earlier. It omits short positions and many smaller managers/positions and reports positions rather than daily transactions. | Free. | High: bulk files, amendments, CUSIP/ticker history, duplicates, filing lag, and manager identity need cleaning. | **Usable for slow, quarterly common-ownership links**, not real-time ownership pressure. As-of filing dates must be respected to avoid look-ahead. [SEC 13F datasets](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/form-13f-data-sets), [13F instructions](https://www.sec.gov/files/form13f.pdf). |
| ETF holdings: SPDR (SPY, DIA, XLK and sector SPDRs) | Issuer fund pages provide daily holdings downloads; sample XLK workbook is saved below. Historical filing route is SEC N-PORT, where available. | Issuer pages are typically current daily holdings, not a guaranteed public long archive. SEC N-PORT structured holdings data runs October 2019–September 2026; it is monthly, published quarterly, and filing publication is lagged. SPY and DIA are unit investment trusts, so N-PORT coverage/history should be checked fund-by-fund rather than assumed. | Free for issuer current holdings and SEC filings. | Low for current files; medium/high for historical N-PORT parsing and point-in-time reconstruction. | **Current membership is easy; robust daily historical network is not.** Use monthly N-PORT with filing/publication dates, or ask the ETF issuers for archived daily files. [SSGA fund finder](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/fund-finder), [SEC N-PORT datasets](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/form-n-port-data-sets). |
| ETF holdings: iShares | BlackRock fund pages expose current holdings downloads; Form N-PORT gives the same lagged monthly filing route for covered funds. | Current daily files; public standardized N-PORT history from Oct 2019 onward at monthly holdings frequency. No general free daily historical archive located. | Free for current page / public filings; historical daily archive may require vendor access or issuer request. | Low current / high historical. | **Current usable; historical daily network uncertain.** [iShares fund search](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/etf-investments). |
| Customer-supplier links: SEC 10-K major-customer disclosures | Inline XBRL/HTML annual reports via EDGAR; identify explicit named customers in concentration-risk and revenue footnotes. Download only filings relevant to the Dow roster and record filing date, fiscal period, customer name and disclosed revenue share. | Annual, issuer-reported, often only customers above a materiality threshold; coverage is sparse and disclosure language is inconsistent. Historical filings are public, subject to parsing and identity resolution. | Free. | Very high manual review and entity resolution; customer names may be withheld as “Customer A.” | **Possible but too sparse for a complete 30-name network**; better as a small, dated subnetwork or event-specific link set. [SEC EDGAR company search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/). |
| Compustat Segments / FactSet supply chain | UNSW Business School documents FactSet Workstation and FactSet Ownership/Lionshares, and separately lists Compustat Global/North America among research databases. Contact the Business School data contact/library for student entitlement. In FactSet, search each issuer in Company/Supply Chain or Segments, set historical period and as-of date, then export the result from the table to Excel/CSV; confirm field definitions, history, and redistribution limits with the data administrator. | FactSet Revere supply-chain relationships are commercial vendor content; Compustat Segments are segment financial disclosures (not automatically a complete customer-supplier graph). Coverage and historical link dates depend on the licensed module. | Included only if UNSW license grants the relevant module; otherwise paid. | Medium if access works; high for links without start/end dates or for segment-to-customer mapping. | **FactSet access is plausible but entitlement is not public proof.** UNSW’s published page supports Workstation and Lionshares; it does not establish that every student account has Revere Supply Chain or segment export access. Verify before designing the test. [UNSW Business School databases](https://www.unsw.edu.au/business/our-research/research-environment/databases). |
| Intraday: yfinance / Yahoo Finance | `yfinance.download(tickers=..., interval='1h'/'5m', start=..., end=...)`; sample no historical pull was retained because the existing project contains only daily files. | Public yfinance wrapper documents intraday retention limits: commonly last 60 days for minute/hour bars (provider behavior can change); intervals include 1m–1h. Yahoo is not a licensed institutional point-in-time feed and symbol/history availability is uneven. | No API fee; terms/availability and reliability are not guaranteed. | Low for current 30-name pull, high for repairing missing bars and validating timestamp/session handling. | **Cannot cover the 2020–26 development window at intraday frequency.** Do not use it to claim an intraday backtest across the project sample. [yfinance download reference](https://ericpien.github.io/yfinance/reference/api/yfinance.download.html). |
| Intraday: Alpha Vantage | REST API; `TIME_SERIES_INTRADAY`, 1/5/15/30/60-minute OHLCV, CSV or JSON; free key. | Its docs describe premium access for historical intraday; premium can request month-specific history back to 2000. Free tier is 25 API requests/day and limits symbol requests; current docs do not promise free historical intraday suitable for this full panel. | Free daily allowance; historical intraday is premium, price depends on plan. | Medium; 30 Dow names at a 25-call/day cap takes at least two days even before history/month pagination. | **Not viable as a free full-sample source.** An education project should request a quote/entitlement first. [Official docs](https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/), [support/limits](https://www.alphavantage.co/support/). |
| Intraday: Polygon (now Massive) | Official REST aggregates endpoint, minute bars; API key required. | Free Stocks Basic currently advertises 2 years historical data, 5 calls/minute, minute aggregates and end-of-day data. This is rolling history, so in Nov 2026 it does not reach most of the 2020–26 project sample. Paid plans extend to 5/10/20+ years depending on plan. | Basic free; Starter listed at US$29/month; higher tiers cost more. | Low for recent panel; medium for multi-name pagination and corporate-action/market-session QA. | **Free tier is insufficient for the full development period; paid coverage may work.** Verify eligibility and terms before relying on historical data. [Official pricing](https://massive.com/pricing?product=stocks), [coverage explanation](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/how-much-historical-stock-data-does-massive-have). |
| Existing project hourly feed | The owned `repo` and `w1_engine` folders contain `pressure_heat_backtest_daily.py` and daily price CSVs, but no hourly companion script, hourly bars, download manifest, provider citation, or source URL. Daily code describes its hourly predecessor's fields/fill logic, not the source vendor. | Unverifiable from the supplied project files. | Unverifiable. | High until the original assignment notebook/data/source is recovered. | **Provenance unresolved.** Do not attribute the hourly feed to Yahoo, Alpha Vantage, or another vendor without the missing file/manifest. |
| Short interest: FINRA | FINRA Equity Short Interest files/API; exchange-listed short interest is collected on settlement dates twice monthly and published after dissemination; use the official historical data, not daily short-sale volume as a substitute. | Broad U.S. equity coverage; bimonthly snapshots, not daily flow. The official FINRA API catalog has equity short-interest data and archives; older data access/field coverage should be confirmed for exact dates. | Free. | Low–medium; map security identifiers, settlement dates, report/delivery lag, and distinguish from daily short-sale volume. | **Useful as a slow state variable or event condition**, not direct daily buy pressure. [FINRA Equity Short Interest](https://otce.finra.org/otce/equityShortInterest), [FINRA API](https://developer.finra.org/products/query-api), [FINRA short-interest explanation](https://syndication.finra.org/content/short-interest-what-it-what-it-not). |
| Options-implied measures | Cboe delayed option chain pages/data are available for current snapshots; Yahoo exposes current expirations/chains. Cboe DataShop sells historical quote files. | Current/delayed chain snapshot only from free pages; there is no demonstrated free historical chain panel for 2020–26. Historical IV/OI/Greeks require licensed archives or a vendor. | Current delayed quotes free; historical option quote data paid. | Medium/high: contract survivorship, expirations, corporate actions, stale quotes, and Greeks matter. | **Free data cannot support a historical options-implied network/predictor test.** Cboe historical data is technically appropriate but likely too costly for this project. [Cboe delayed quotes](https://www.cboe.com/delayed_quotes/), [Cboe DataShop option quote intervals](https://datashop.cboe.com/option-quote-intervals). |
| Analyst coverage / revisions | FactSet estimates or I/B/E/S through UNSW-approved research access; access page lists I/B/E/S and FactSet in the research environment. Public brokerage recommendations are not a reliable complete historical consensus file. Export historical estimates/coverage only through licensed interfaces, preserving timestamp and fiscal-period fields. | Daily/weekly revisions depending on vendor; broad public-company coverage, but historical coverage and analyst identifiers depend on license. | Institutional subscription; possibly available to approved UNSW researchers, not confirmed for every student. | Medium; point-in-time vintages, estimate horizons, stale forecasts, and analyst IDs must be controlled. | **Potentially feasible through UNSW license after access confirmation; not free.** [UNSW databases](https://www.unsw.edu.au/business/our-research/research-environment/databases). |
| Fund flows | Free daily ETF price/NAV and shares outstanding from issuer/SEC reports can support approximate primary-market creation/redemption flows; commercial FactSet/Morningstar flows provide standardized estimates. SEC N-PORT is monthly, not daily flow. | Daily for some issuer NAV/share data; standardized fund-flow history depends on paid vendor. Need differentiate flows from market-price changes and fund distributions. | Free raw filings/issuer data; commercial flow history paid. | High to reconstruct from shares outstanding × NAV and reconcile splits/distributions; low–medium if vendor access. | **Possible for ETFs with available shares-outstanding history, but daily flow history is not a universal free feed.** N-PORT alone is too sparse for a daily pressure test. [SEC N-PORT](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/form-n-port-data-sets), [SSGA fund pages](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/fund-finder). |
