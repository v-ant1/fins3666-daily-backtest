Audited 10 November 2026. I reopened each DOI/URL shown on Cards 1–16. Publisher and institutional pages were read where accessible; where DOI redirects returned 403/500 or an access barrier, I checked the accessible primary author/university PDF when available. “Directly verified” below means title/authors/year were compared with the source and any numerical assertion was checked at its cited abstract/table/text; a failed fetch is recorded rather than treated as a successful verification.

## Summary and remaining source limits

The citation metadata and the numerical claims in Cards 1–4, 8, 11–12 and 16 were directly checked against the linked primary full text or publisher page. Cards 5–6, 9–10, 13–15 rely on accessible primary versions opened in the preceding source review; fetch errors and version boundaries are stated above. Card 7 is downgraded because its exact percentage claim could not be checked. Card 3 is corrected to the published Table V result; Cards 8, 14 and 15 now distinguish working-paper estimates from journal-version claims.

No evidence card is treated as a recent net-of-cost result for Dow 30 stocks. The cited research mostly uses broad CRSP samples, monthly frequencies, pre-2010 data, or non-equity asset markets. This audit did not run a backtest or access outcome data.
