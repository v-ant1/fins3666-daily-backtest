## 4. Free fallback data

- **Issuer pages:** fund sponsors may provide current holdings, NAV and some shares-outstanding history (example: [SSGA fund finder](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/fund-finder)). L4 downloaded a current XLK holdings workbook; it proves current access only. Historical archives vary by issuer and ETF.
- **SEC Form N-PORT:** SEC’s [N-PORT data sets](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/form-n-port-data-sets) and EDGAR provide structured fund portfolio holdings. The SEC dataset covers October 2019–September 2026 and is updated quarterly, while holdings are reported for each month-end. This helps late holdout and most development but not 2010–19, and is not daily flow or shares outstanding. SEC delayed its new monthly public-reporting schedule until November 2027 for larger fund groups and May 2028 for smaller groups, so publication lag remains relevant ([SEC rule notice](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2025/04/s7-26-22)).
- **SEC Form N-CEN:** annual registered-fund reports can help identify funds and attributes. SEC filing examples are annual reports, not daily shares-flow records ([filing example](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1592900/000119312526388859/0001193125-26-388859-index.htm)). Complete daily historical shares outstanding is **not verified**.
- **SEC Form 13F:** free quarterly EDGAR institutional holdings. The SEC says reports include shares and market value at quarter-end and cover eligible Section 13(f) securities, including some ETFs ([SEC FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-13f)). It is not transaction-level, has a filing delay, omits short positions, and does not provide mutual-fund TNA or flow.
- **Approximate ETF flow:** if an issuer provides daily shares outstanding and NAV, calculate creations/redemptions from share-count changes, value at a stated NAV, and adjust for splits/distributions. This is a reconstruction proposal, not a universal free feed. AUM change alone confounds investment returns with net flows.

## 5. Minimum data and deadline realism

All dossiers also need daily Dow prices, point-in-time roster/security mapping, and both development dates (2020-11-27–2026-10-30) and sealed holdout (2010-01-01–2020-10-31). L4 checked availability only: 37/40 windows were available; KFT, DWDP and WBA remain unresolved, and UTX is represented by an RTX lineage proxy. Do not silently fill gaps. Effort assumes one student, coding experience, prompt lecturer support and a reproducible extraction.

|---|---|---:|---|
| **D1: Retail signed flow** | Timestamped trades and best quotes, venue and classifier inputs for every Dow member, daily returns and contemporaneous roster; TAQ must contain the inputs used by the specified retail classifier. | 4–6 weeks for access, extraction, cleaning, symbol changes and classification. | **No on current evidence.** TAQ spans both date windows, but team access and field sufficiency are unconfirmed; high-frequency processing is substantial. |

| **D3: Mutual-fund common-owner flow** | Monthly point-in-time fund TNA and total returns to estimate net flow, plus dated fund security holdings/weights to form the common-owner network, for 2010–20 and 2020–26. Candidate: CRSP Mutual Funds plus Thomson Reuters Mutual Funds holdings linked with MFLinks, or FactSet Ownership after coverage confirmation. | 3–5 weeks if the CRSP and holdings modules are immediately accessible; 5–8 weeks if filings must be collected/parsed. | **No for full holdout replication now.** UNSW documents CRSP variables and history from 1962, but this team’s undergraduate workflow, access to the linked holdings table and extract completeness are not confirmed. 13F alone is not a substitute. |

Before promising any full-period test, get entitlement and field/date coverage in writing, then inventory identifiers, observation dates, frequency and filing/publication timestamps without opening sealed-period outcome values. Current evidence supports a possible development-only pilot after coordinator approval, not a complete holdout-ready dataset.
