# Pre-registration: inverse heat connectivity Round 3

## Frozen selection rule (written before running selection jobs)

I will compare seven fixed long-only strategies on only Dow and cross-asset selection dates 2020-11-27 through 2022-12-30. Each uses a 63-session correlation window, positive top-five undirected graph, unit heat diffusion time, next-open fills, actual traded-notional costs of 5 bp per side, and 21-session rebalancing unless the variant explicitly specifies another frequency. The seven candidates are: (1) inverse connectivity power k=1 across all 30 names; (2) inverse connectivity power k=2 across all names; (3) equal weight among the 15 least-connected names; (4) inverse connectivity among the 10 least-connected names; (5) inverse connectivity among the 15 least-connected names; (6) among the 15 least-connected names, weight by inverse connectivity times inverse volatility times volatility-scaled 21-session reversal; and (7) the same as (6) with 63-session rebalancing. The reversal score is negative 21-session total return divided by trailing 63-session daily volatility times sqrt(21), centered cross-sectionally, clipped to [-2,2], exponentiated at half-strength, then multiplied into equal least-connected-name weights.

Select the eligible candidate with the highest simple average of its Dow and ETF daily Sharpe ratios, provided its mean actual notional turnover is at most 50% per rebalance in each universe. Ties break by higher average information ratio versus schedule-matched EW30, then smaller average maximum drawdown magnitude, then lower turnover. EW30 is the fallback if no candidate qualifies. No test data will be used to choose or alter the frozen strategy. No-trade bands, alternative graph densities, and diffusion times are not added after observing selection results; they remain future research ideas under the 12-variant cap.

This screen contains seven candidate definitions; each tested once in each of two selection universes (14 portfolio-series evaluations), with EW30 benchmark series. The cumulative trial count for deflated Sharpe reporting is 85 (prior approximate 70 + eight Round 2 portfolios + seven candidates in this screen); close relatives make the independent-trial approximation imperfect.
