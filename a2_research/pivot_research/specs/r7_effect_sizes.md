# R7 literature effect sizes for the seven frozen R6 mechanisms

**Scope.** Literature review only. No R6 run/result files, project return data, or holdout data were used. The target is a published cross-sectional effect in U.S. large-cap stocks with observations after 2010; where the closest result is older, foreign, an aggregate-market result, or a different outcome, that mismatch is explicit rather than treated as evidence for the frozen signal.

## Conversion convention

Several papers report long-short/quintile spreads but not a cross-sectional rank IC. For a transparent rough normalization, I use

`approximate rank IC ≈ monthly long-short spread / (2.8 × monthly cross-sectional return SD)`.

The factor 2.8 is the normal-score mean difference between the top and bottom quintiles. Since source papers seldom report a matched long-short spread SD, the common reference is **11.31% monthly**, obtained from the 39.18% average annual single-stock standard deviation reported in Lachance (2023), divided by √12. It is a stock-level time-series proxy, not an estimate of cross-sectional monthly SD or of any strategy’s long-short volatility; the resulting denominator is 31.668% monthly. These are pseudo-ICs, not reported rank correlations, and should not be compared as if the portfolios, holding periods, signals, and universes matched. If a source gives only annual returns or a non-return coefficient, I mark conversion unavailable or explicitly label any mechanical rescaling as non-comparable.

## Interpretation

These conversions are screening-scale comparisons only. Of the seven, the nearest numerically documented, post-2010 large-cap evidence is for shorting flow (D1), connected-peer propagation (E1), broad value-weighted reversal (J1), and overnight return persistence (P1); none is an exact replication of its R6 frozen signal, universe, horizon, or endpoint. G1 lacks a qualifying post-2010 U.S. mega-cap effect, K1’s adjacent announcement-month premium is not PEAD, and R1’s reported premium mostly predates 2010. No literature effect is asserted to pass R7’s power, cost, or portfolio criteria.

- D1: `0.0026 / (2.8 × 0.1131) = 0.0082`.
- E1: `0.0140 / (2.8 × 0.1131) = 0.0442`.
- J1: `0.0054 / (2.8 × 0.1131) = 0.0171`.
- K1 adjacent earnings-month premium: `0.00485 / (2.8 × 0.1131) = 0.0153` (not a PEAD estimate).
- P1 mechanical annual-to-month scaling: `(0.3750 − (−0.1168)) / 12 / (2.8 × 0.1131) = 0.0129`; not a true rank IC.
- R1: `0.00276 / (2.8 × 0.1131) = 0.0087`.
