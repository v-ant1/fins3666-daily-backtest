### Test L-DEVEV-01 — S&P additions

- Event definition: a ticker's `effective_from` date in the dated S&P PIT roster is its addition effective date. Retain events whose effective date and all required price windows lie within the development period, and for which the event ticker and daily PIT S&P benchmark have valid adjusted closes. Announcement dates will be added only if linked to a freely accessible, dated S&P DJI release that is available from staged roster references; otherwise use effective-date-only windows and explicitly report announcement-to-effective CAR unavailable. No announcement-date proxy will be inferred.
- Daily stock return: adjusted-close close-to-close simple return `P[i,t]/P[i,t-1]-1`. Daily benchmark: equal-weight mean of same-day returns for S&P PIT members at both closes, excluding the event ticker. Daily abnormal return is stock return minus this benchmark return.
- Event CAR: arithmetic sum of daily abnormal returns over the explicitly inclusive session windows `[announcement session..effective session]` if a dated announcement is available, `[effective−5 trading sessions..effective session]` (six closes), and `[effective+1..effective+10 trading sessions]` (ten closes, reversal diagnostic). Report per-window usable N; do not fill missing bars. If announcement date unavailable, report that window N/A. Mean and cross-event sample SD (`ddof=1`) are across event CARs. 95% CI for each mean is two-sided Student-t interval `mean ± t(.975,N−1)*SD/sqrt(N)`; disclose same-date event clustering and interpret this interval as event-level descriptive/inferential approximation.
- Required power comparison: primary 2016-01-04..2020-10-30 S&P holdout planning scenario, N=96 additions, MDE=`2.970*measured development cross-event SD/sqrt(96)` for each corresponding window. Footnote only: 2010-01-04..2020-10-30, N=216, same formula. Compare event-window development mean (noisy) and published post-2010 +0.8% effect; no claim that development estimate validates the published holdout effect.

### Test L-DEVEV-03 — FINRA days-to-cover monthly rank IC

- Formation dates: final trading session of each calendar month in the development period for which next month-end adjusted closes are within 2020-11-27..2026-10-30. Universe: S&P PIT members present on formation and next-month end, with finite positive DTC and valid month-end adjusted closes.
- Publication lag: the panel has settlement date but no publication timestamp. Treat each FINRA semi-monthly settlement observation as publicly usable only from the seventh U.S. business day after settlement (conservative FINRA release lag); at each month-end use the latest observation with that assumed availability date on or before the formation close. No same-settlement-day or future panel observation is usable. DTC is the panel's supplied `days_to_cover` value. Signal is `-DTC`; outcome is next-calendar-month adjusted-close total price return (month-end close to next month-end close; dividends are not available in this bundle and thus not included). Compute monthly cross-sectional Spearman rank IC, its mean, Newey–West t-statistic with Bartlett kernel and 3 monthly lags, and mean IC by calendar year. Months with fewer than 30 eligible names are omitted and counted.
- Required power comparison: primary 2016-01-04..2020-10-30 planning sample ≈58 monthly formations; recompute 80%-power required mean IC as `2.970 * SD(monthly IC) / sqrt(58)` where SD uses sample dispersion across development monthly ICs. Report the corresponding 2010-2020 ≈130-month value only as a footnote, using the same measured SD. Compare to the published 1.19% monthly low-minus-high DTC spread as a non-matched external magnitude; clearly label the development IC and SD as noisy exploratory estimates and the effect mapping as non-equivalent.
