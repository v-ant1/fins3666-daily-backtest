## 4. S&P PIT membership, missing histories, delistings and holdout

### PIT membership quality

The working S&P roster, `r6_data/sp500_pit_roster.csv`, has 623 membership intervals over 622 ticker identities. It is a reconstructed historical roster based on Wikipedia historical/current constituent tables, replayed changes and S&P DJI releases, not an authoritative complete PIT vendor history. The QA records 132 roster-change rows, 130 directly supported by S&P DJI releases, and documents the WLTW→WTW and CDAY→DAY ticker lineages plus issuer-confirmed SATS→ECHO and EQR→VMRK transitions. Membership intervals use inclusive start and exclusive end dates.

### The 51-name price-history gap and delistings

`r6_data/QA.md` and the current D1/E1 inputs identify 51 historical S&P ticker identities with no complete price history in the final gapfilled panel:

`ABMD, ALXN, ANSS, ATVI, AVB, CDAY, CERN, CMA, CTLT, CTRA, CTXS, CXO, DAY, DFS, DISCA, DISCK, DISH, DRE, EA, ETFC, FLIR, FRC, GPS, HBI, HES, HFC, HOLX, INFO, IPG, JNPR, JWN, K, KSU, KVUE, LEG, MRO, MXIM, NBL, NLSN, PBCT, PXD, QRVO, SBNY, SEE, SIVB, TIF, TWTR, VAR, WBA, WBD, XLNX`.

Many correspond to acquired, merged, renamed or delisted histories; examples include ABMD, ATVI, EA, SIVB, TWTR and WBA. That classification does not certify that every missing provider history is a delisting. No current-company/acquirer price was substituted for a missing predecessor. The coverage panel reports 775,662 total S&P rows in the final gapfilled parquet; E1’s filtered runner input has 775,398 daily member rows. D1 and E1 have distinct date/member eligibility denominators and should not be treated as the same missingness statistic.

There is a stale-count discrepancy: G1’s earlier QA says 56 S&P symbols have no bars (566/622 have any rows), while the current general QA and D1/E1 manifests report 51 unresolved price-history identities. G1’s input manifest still pins its own bundle and coverage hash; treat the difference as a version/definition discrepancy requiring reconciliation, not as evidence that five delisted histories have been recovered. Dow gaps are also documented separately: E1 uses dated membership, with 43,944 of 44,640 scheduled development member-days having complete OHLCV and dated SIC; G1’s legacy Dow file list has 33 daily files and no files for GOOGL, PFE, RTX and XOM, with gapfill rows separately staged.

## Provenance and interpretation notes

Counts above come from existing `QA.md` files, runner input/build manifests, coverage tables and file metadata. The extant runner manifests give aggregate coverage and formation-date gates; they do not provide a consistent year-by-year missingness table for every source, so no annual figures have been inferred. For sealed data, only manifest/QA content and filenames were consulted. “Adjusted” distinguishes adjusted prices from raw OHLC and split-restated volume; dividend adjustment is not implied for volume. “Known after close” is an input-timing convention, not a guarantee of same-close executability. The signal/publication-time column flags when a runner can first use its input under the documented protocol and identifies timing that the source files do not actually prove.
