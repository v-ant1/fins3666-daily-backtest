# D5 pre-freeze audit

**Scope:** read-only review of `specs/r4_prereg/D5.md`. No prices, returns, signals, SIC coverage counts, pair counts, or D5 outcomes were computed. The file is not frozen; the points below are specification review only.

## Findings and proposed replacements

| Priority | Finding | Exact replacement text |
|---|---|---|
| **BLOCKER** | The cost helper's turnover input must be benchmark-relative total-notional turnover, but D5 caps the strategy's total turnover at 50% of NAV. Those are not the same denominator. The numeric 0.0057591 hurdle is valid only if 0.50 is explicitly the benchmark-relative planning turnover. | “For planning, define benchmark-relative total-notional turnover as the fixed 0.50 NAV per rebalance input to `break_even_rank_ic(0.50, 0.03007510102898867, cost_per_side=0.0005, top_fraction=1/6) = 0.0057591`. This is a planning assumption, not measured D5 turnover and not the strategy's separate 50% turnover limit. Report actual strategy and EW30 traded notional and realized costs from the actual-trade engine. Require gross active edge at least four times realized costs.” Keep the 50% strategy turnover cap but label it as such, separately from helper turnover. |
| **BLOCKER** | Holm is required across the whole goal, and D5 says the family includes sensitivity tests, but it does not enumerate D5's exact endpoints. Variant 3 also duplicates the reverse-edge placebo. | “The D5 endpoints entered into the complete D4–D6 Holm family are: (1) variant 1, 20-session primary five-session follower IC (one-sided Newey–West p-value); (2) reverse-edge mean IC (two-sided Newey–West p-value); (3) observed-versus-within-SIC permuted-leader placebo comparison (one-sided randomization p-value); (4) observed-versus-20-session-shifted-graph placebo comparison (one-sided randomization p-value); (5) variant 2, 60-session five-session follower IC (one-sided Newey–West p-value); and (6) variant 1 net portfolio excess return versus EW30 (one-sided block-bootstrap p-value). Report raw and Holm-adjusted p-values for each. Count the reverse-edge calculation once as a placebo, not as a separate rescue variant. The coordinator records the full D4–D6 endpoint family in `specs/r4_trials.md` before any outcome is computed.” |
| **SHOULD-FIX** | The minimum-pair rule can pass with 10 edges from only one large SIC group and one leader. That is a weak feasibility condition for a network propagation test, even though it is checked before outcomes. Add a simple input-only breadth requirement. | “At each eligible fifth-session formation date, count distinct ordered stock pairs, distinct leader firms, and distinct two-digit SIC groups represented by an edge. D5 is infeasible before outcome access if fewer than 10 distinct pairs, fewer than 3 leaders, or fewer than 3 SIC groups are present on more than 20% of eligible dates. Report all three counts and distributions. Do not relax these thresholds after the coverage check.” |

Tighten the timestamp boundary and price convention so filing day or split adjustment cannot be interpreted differently by the implementer. The t signal at close t and next-open fill otherwise avoid look-ahead. | Replace the SIC timing sentence with: “For a signal on trading session t, use the latest issuer 10-K header SIC with an SEC acceptance timestamp no later than 16:00 America/New_York on the immediately preceding NYSE session. Eligible-observation and effective-date counts are reported as observed input coverage, not selected or tuned.” Keep the dated SIC panel, 20-session primary and 60-session sensitivity definitions fixed. |

## Overall assessment

The SIC availability cutoff and the 20-session dollar-volume lookback are already framed to avoid look-ahead: the SIC is known by the prior session's close, and volume data end at t−1. The main pre-freeze blockers are the mismatch between the cost-helper turnover definition and the portfolio limit, and the missing explicit D5 endpoint list for the goal-wide Holm family. The pair-count and placebo wording should also be tightened before freeze. This audit does not authorize any D5 signal or return calculation.
